## Style

- Do not use em dashes (—) in prose, docs, commit messages, or UI copy. Prefer commas, colons, periods, parentheses, or separate sentences. En-dashes in numeric ranges (e.g. 2–3) are fine.
- Icons: use `lucide-react`. Icon-only controls need an accessible name (`aria-label`); decorative badge glyphs stay `aria-hidden`.
